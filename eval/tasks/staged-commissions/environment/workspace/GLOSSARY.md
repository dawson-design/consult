# Glossary

Use these terms in code, the API, and docs. Customers and staff often use the
informal words in brackets; translate them.

- **Commission** (order): a customer's order for one or more pieces.
- **Piece**: one catalogue design in a commission, with a quantity.
- **Design**: a catalogue entry, named by its code, such as `oak-bench`.
- **Patron** (customer, user): a person with an account.
- **On the bench** (in progress, being built): building has started.
- **Consignment** (tracking number): the carrier's reference for a dispatched
  commission.
- **Withdrawal** (cancellation): a patron calling off a commission before
  dispatch.
- **Restocking fee**: what a patron owes for a withdrawal after building
  starts.
