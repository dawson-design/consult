# Returning customers

We want to thank customers who come back. Once a customer has had an order
delivered, every order they place after that gets 10% off.

- A commission shows its `discount`, in the same money format as `total`, and
  its `total` is the price after the discount.
- The deposit, the balance, the restocking fee, and the refund all work from
  that total.
- An order keeps the price it was placed at. A delivery doesn't change orders
  the customer has already placed.
- These amounts follow the requested currency, like `total`.

Work in the provided repository.
